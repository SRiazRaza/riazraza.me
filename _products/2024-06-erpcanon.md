## Overview

ERPCanon is an enterprise-grade ERP solution designed for modern businesses seeking to streamline operations, improve efficiency, and make data-driven decisions. Built with scalability and ease-of-use in mind, it provides a unified platform for managing all critical business functions.

## Core Modules

**📦 Inventory Management**
- Real-time stock tracking and level monitoring
- Automated reorder point management
- Multi-warehouse support with transfer capabilities
- Barcode scanning and RFID integration
- Inventory forecasting and analytics

**💰 Sales & Order Management**
- Quote-to-cash workflow automation
- Customer relationship management (CRM) integration
- Order processing with intelligent routing
- Pricing rules engine and discount management
- Sales performance analytics and dashboards

**👥 Human Resources**
- Comprehensive employee database and records
- Payroll processing and tax compliance
- Leave and attendance management
- Performance evaluation workflows
- Recruitment and onboarding tools

**📊 Financial Management**
- General ledger and multi-currency support
- Accounts payable and receivable automation
- Budget planning and variance analysis
- Financial reporting and consolidation
- Cash flow forecasting

**🚚 Supply Chain**
- Procurement planning and vendor management
- Purchase order automation
- Supplier collaboration portal
- Logistics and shipping coordination
- Supply chain visibility and analytics

## Key Features

✨ **Modular Architecture** - Deploy only the modules you need and scale as your business grows
