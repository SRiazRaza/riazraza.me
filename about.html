My Story

Originally from Pakistan, I moved to Italy to pursue my MSc in Computer Science at the University of Padova, specialising in Artificial Intelligence. My thesis on 3D Human Pose Estimation from Egocentric Videos of Social Interactions took me to IRI-UPC in Barcelona, where I collaborated with leading researchers on egocentric vision using the Microsoft HoloLens.

I'm fascinated by the intersection of deep learning, perception, and user-facing products — whether that's building SaaS tools, writing about AI, or experimenting with new architectures. I also run ERPCanon, a suite of enterprise SaaS modules.

Outside of work: obsessive manga reader (JoJo's, Berserk, Vinland Saga), retro game enjoyer, and occasional pasta chef. ⛏
